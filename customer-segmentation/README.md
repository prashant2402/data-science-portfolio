# 🛍️ Customer Segmentation with Clustering

This project applies unsupervised machine learning to segment customers into meaningful groups based on their purchase behavior and value. Developed as part of the **University of Cambridge Data Science Career Accelerator**, the project is designed to support **targeted marketing**, **customer retention**, and **lifecycle optimization** strategies.

---

## 📊 Project Objective

To identify high-value, at-risk, and growth-potential customer segments using clustering techniques — enabling data-driven marketing strategies tailored to each group.

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

We identified **three key customer segments**:

1. **Power Shoppers** – High purchase frequency + CLV; retention risk due to low recency  
2. **Regular Customers** – Stable, mid-value segment; potential to upgrade  
3. **Occasional Shoppers** – Low frequency/value but recent engagement; prime for reactivation

> 📈 Potential business impact: 15–30% increase in CLV and 20–25% improvement in marketing ROI through segment-specific targeting

---

## 🔬 Methods Used

- **Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering**
- **K-Means Clustering**
- **Elbow Method & Silhouette Analysis**
- **Hierarchical Clustering & Dendrograms**
- **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)**
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) scoring**
